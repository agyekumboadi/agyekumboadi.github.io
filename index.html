A little about me

Technical thinking, grounded in operations.

I enjoy the space where data, technology and people meet - understanding a problem, designing a practical approach and making the result usable.

How I approach problems

My background combines information technology, data analytics and IT security. I am particularly interested in analytics that can be explained, automation that makes work more consistent, and systems that treat information responsibly.

Alongside technical projects, my operational experience has shown me why solutions need to work for the people using them. A good dashboard, model or workflow should make a real task clearer, safer or more efficient.
